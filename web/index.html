COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI detectors prove beyond doubt that text was AI-generated?
No. AI detectors evaluate statistical patterns such as vocabulary entropy and transition markers. While AegisText is calibrated to provide realistic posterior probabilities, statistical models should be treated as one piece of evidence rather than absolute proof.
How does AegisText defeat AI humanizers and zero-width spaces?
Before text enters the feature extraction pipeline, AegisText executes a preprocessing pass that strips invisible zero-width spaces (`\u200b`, `\u200c`) and normalizes confusable Cyrillic/Greek homoglyphs to canonical Latin characters.
Are my submitted documents stored or shared?
No. Submissions are processed transiently in memory for inference and are never saved to long-term databases or used for training.
