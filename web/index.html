METHODOLOGY & GUIDELINES

Assessment Guide: Evidence, Not a Verdict

1. The Principle of Uncertainty

Machine learning text detectors cannot prove authorship beyond doubt. Statistical models evaluate stylistic regularity, vocabulary distribution, and transition entropy. These measures indicate similarity to synthetic training corpora, not legal certainty.

2. Interpreting Linguistic Signals

Human writing exhibits high burstiness: sentences vary naturally in length, cadence, and vocabulary choice. In contrast, language model outputs often feature uniform token predictability and high discourse transition marker density.

3. Adversarial Robustness

AegisText incorporates proactive unicode sanitization, neutralizing zero-width spaces (\u200b) and confusable homoglyphs before evaluation.
