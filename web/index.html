System Architecture

AegisText combines an 84-dimensional linguistic feature extraction pipeline with an isotonic-calibrated ensemble classifier. Features encompass:

  • Stylometry: Type-token ratio, Shannon vocabulary entropy, punctuation rhythm, hapax legomena ratio.
  • Structural Discourse: Sentence length variance, discourse transition marker density, paragraph structural coherence.
  • Predictability & Burstiness: Frequency rank zipfian slope, lexical repetition cycles, burstiness distribution.
  • Semantic Flow: Consecutive sentence Jaccard similarity and cross-sentence semantic drift.
